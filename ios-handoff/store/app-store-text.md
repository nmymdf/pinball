## Description (English)
A full vintage American coin-op pinball machine with a Wild West train-robbery theme.

• Five colored steam bumpers that upgrade to silver and gold as you hit them
• Timed missions: Coal & Water, Freight Line, Loop Express, Switch Yard… finish them to start the three-ball Great Robbery multiball
• Light up the wanted posters, complete the railway route and spell T-R-A-I-N for big prizes
• Fall into the Vault to ride a train car: hit the TNT barrels, knock down bandits, grab coins and crack the safe
• Skill shot, combos, bonus multiplier, steam boost, mystery awards and a free game at one million points
• Portrait touch controls with big flipper buttons, easy to play with one hand
• Traditional Chinese and English
• No internet, no account, no ads

The "INSERT COIN / CREDIT" text is only the arcade machine's virtual game counter; there is no real money, betting or purchase of any kind. All content is bundled in the app. To play: tap START GAME on the home screen, then use the large buttons at the bottom (left flipper, right flipper, launch).
